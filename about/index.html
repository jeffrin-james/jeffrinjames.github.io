engineer × designer

The engineer in me deals with the reasoning, precision and technicalities while the designer in me, gives it the emotional depth. I believe these personal superhero traits help me to lead with logic and influence with emotion.

Growing up reading comic books, watching a lot of cartoons, playing the same characters on PlayStation and trying to make my versions of these characters behind notebooks - that's when I sub-consciously started to incline towards art, literature, and well, anything creative.
